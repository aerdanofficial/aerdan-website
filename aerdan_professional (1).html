01 — THE STORY

Built beyond
ordinary.

AERDAN is a premium streetwear label from Shillong, built around restraint, movement and the pursuit of a higher standard.

“There is a certain elegance in becoming.”

AERDAN began with a simple idea: clothing should feel like part of the person wearing it, not a costume. Clean silhouettes, considered weight and quiet details create pieces made to live in.

From Shillong, where the mountains are part of the horizon, AERDAN carries that same sense of ascent into its design. The peak is not a destination. It is the standard you keep moving toward.

THE PEAK IS
THE STANDARD.Not louder. Not busier. Better considered. AERDAN is made for people who keep refining who they are.
